# fixtures/federalist -- The Federalist Papers, 85 files

The 85 essays of "The Federalist" (Hamilton, Jay and Madison, 1787-1788),
one plain-text file each, for the DEMO-FEDGREP screencast
(site/demos/fedgrep.py, docs/DEMO-FEDGREP.md). They are generated by
`python3 scripts/build_federalist.py pg1404.txt` and committed so the
gate does not need the network; `--check` verifies the committed files
against the source.

## Status

The text is by authors dead for over two centuries and was published in
1787-1788: public domain everywhere. Only the text between the
`*** START` and `*** END` markers is kept (the Project Gutenberg header,
licence and trailer are removed, so none of that licence text is in the
tree), which is the text of the papers and the author's own footnotes.

## Transformations (all of them)

1. Cut to the text between the `*** START OF` and `*** END OF` lines.
2. CRLF to LF; trailing blanks removed from every line; blank lines at
   the end of a paper dropped; one final LF.
3. Dropped: the title page before No. 1 ("THE FEDERALIST PAPERS / By
   Alexander Hamilton, John Jay, and James Madison").
4. Split at each line `FEDERALIST No. N` into `NO.NN` (85 files, N from
   1 to 85). A paper's file starts with that line; the notes that follow
   the last paper in the source belong to it (No. 85).
5. Non-ASCII folded to ASCII, two characters on two lines, both e acute
   (U+00E9), folded to `e`:
   - No. 6, line 250: `l'Abb\u00e9 de Mably` -> `l'Abbe de Mably`
   - No. 19, line 221: `Abr\u00e9g.` -> `Abreg.`
   Nothing else is changed: spelling, punctuation and `--` dashes are the
   source's.

## What the text is, and is not

The headings are the EDITION's, not the authors' own. This edition heads
the disputed papers (49-58, 62-63) plain `MADISON`, and Nos. 18-20
`MADISON, with HAMILTON`; the headings (title, date and author lines)
are editorial. The E-notes ("E1. Two versions of
this paragraph appear in different editions.", in NO.36 line 285, NO.56
line 172, NO.68 line 157, NO.70 line 338 and NO.77 line 198) are the
editor's, not the authors'. Nothing in the demo claims more than "this
edition heads it MADISON".

Largest paper: No. 83, 34,200 bytes, 558 lines. Longest line: 73
characters. All 85 together: 1,147,199 bytes. `cat NO.*` sha256:
`74daaa544caddebc90775b0d69809642316f1be9b94f05e807147d6915db4d19`.

maux ed does NOT hold No. 83 whole: ed sizes its heap at run time (sbrk up
to $BF00; the 36,480-byte TEXTBUF_SIZE is retired). Measured at 63fb62c2:
462 lines of No. 83 (28,613 bytes) load, 463 lines (28,682 bytes) print
`?` (docs/DEMO-FEDGREP-NOTES.md sec 3A). scripts/build_federalist.py
reports papers above that bound; it does not assert it.
