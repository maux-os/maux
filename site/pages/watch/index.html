Watch

Every film here is made by a script that drives the machine the way a person does, a key at a time. The same script, run without recording, is one of the tests: if what the film shows stops being true, the test fails before anyone films it again.

The video plays at the speed the machine runs. Any stretch that is sped up carries a visible badge. Control keys are named in a strip under the screen, so ^Z and ESC are not invisible.
