A Unix on an Apple IIe, in its first minute. The demo lists the disk, counts the programs in /bin, runs a pipeline through three of them, and runs ps. The BANK column of ps is the whole idea: every process is a 64K RamWorks bank, and the bank number is the process number.

The gate that proves it

This film is made by site/demos/firstmin.py. Run as assertions, the same file is {{gen:demogate}}, and it runs with the rest of the tests. It requires that ls prints the volume root, in order, as the disk image holds it; that wc -l counts exactly the programs the image holds in /bin; that the pipeline prints the last five names, in order; that ps prints distinct PIDs and distinct banks; and that every process sits in the bank that is its PID. Every expected value is read from the disk image by a2til on the host, never typed into the demo. The checks themselves were tested: with one program removed from /bin the count is off by one and the demo fails at "wc counts exactly the files on the disk"; with sort replaced by a copy of cat it fails at the pipeline; with ps replaced by a copy of echo it fails at "ps prints its header".
