A job runs on console 1, a loop that appends a tick to a log every five seconds. chvt 2 moves to a second console with a shell of its own, where vi is used and quit. chvt 1 comes back to the same screen, and the log shows the job kept ticking the whole time.

The gate that proves it

This film is made by site/demos/twocons.py. Run as assertions, the same file is {{gen:demogate}}, and it runs with the rest of the tests. It requires that console 1’s screen, snapshotted before chvt 2, comes back exactly, followed by the chvt 2 line and a fresh prompt; that console 2 starts fresh and its file is written; that the log read on console 2 and the log read on console 1 after the job is killed are both ticks one to k in order, with at least two more ticks in the second, so the job ran while console 2 was in use; and that the loop had not finished. A variant that stops the job for the whole interval was run and is red. With sleep replaced by a copy of true the demo fails at the Terminated notice; with chvt replaced by true it fails at "console 2 is a fresh, blank screen with a prompt".
