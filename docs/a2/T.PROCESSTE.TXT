processtest.s -- task #12 acceptance image.

The boot kernel starts one parent process in bank 1. That parent
exercises the public syscall surface only:
  - spawn("E", argv, NULL), wait() round-trips status 7
  - pipe(), spawn reader/writer with fdactions dup/close, parent closes its
    copies, then wait() reaps statuses $42 and $24
Results live in bank 1 at $0980+ for scripts/check_process.py.
